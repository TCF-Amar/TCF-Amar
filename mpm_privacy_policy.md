# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026  

**Solutions Through Empowering Processes** ("we," "us," "our," or the "Company") operates **STEP Mental Elev8tion** (the "Platform"), a multi-role mental performance training platform connecting athletes, trainers, coaches, and parents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

---

## 1. Who This Policy Applies To

The Platform supports several types of users, each with different data and access levels:

* **Athletes** — Complete assessments, follow training programs, message stakeholders, and join video sessions.
* **Trainers** — Manage athletes, build or generate training programs, access analytics, and communicate with athletes and other stakeholders.
* **Parents/Guardians** — View high-level progress summaries for athletes under their care.
* **Coaches** — View high-level progress summaries for athletes on their team or under their supervision.

This Policy applies to all users regardless of role, with additional protections described in [Section 9](#9-childrens-privacy-and-minor-athletes) for minors.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

* **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, password, role (athlete, trainer, parent, coach), and organizational/team affiliation.
* **Assessment Data:** Responses to the 90-question mental performance assessment and any follow-up assessments.
* **Program & Progress Data:** Training program selections, activity completion, progress notes, goals, and performance metrics.
* **Communications:** Messages exchanged between athletes, trainers, coaches, and parents through the Platform's secure messaging feature.
* **Video Session Data:** Participation in and, where applicable, recordings or metadata (date, time, duration, participants) of video coaching sessions.
* **Media:** Photos, videos, or documents uploaded as part of training content or program materials.
* **Payment Information:** If applicable, billing details processed through our third-party payment processor (we do not store full payment card numbers).
* **Support Requests:** Information you provide when contacting customer support.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

* **Device & Usage Data:** IP address, device type, operating system, browser type, unique device identifiers, app version, and general location (derived from IP address).
* **Log Data:** Access times, pages/screens viewed, features used, crash reports, and diagnostic data.
* **Cookies & Similar Technologies:** Used on our website and, where applicable, in-app to remember preferences and analyze usage (see [Section 8](#8-cookies-and-tracking-technologies)).

### 2.3 Information From Third Parties

* **Trainers, Coaches, or Organizations:** May add or import athlete information (e.g., name, team, contact details) when creating accounts on an athlete's behalf.
* **AI Program Generation:** Inputs used to generate training programs (e.g., assessment results, stated goals) may be processed by AI tools as described in [Section 5](#5-ai-generated-program-content).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
* Create and manage user accounts across all roles.
* Deliver assessments, generate and administer 20-week training programs.
* Enable progress tracking, analytics, and reporting for trainers.
* Provide high-level progress visibility to parents and coaches.
* Facilitate secure messaging between stakeholders.
* Host and support live video coaching sessions.
* Schedule activities and sessions.
* Generate or assist in generating training programs using AI tools.
* Maintain platform security, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service.
* Comply with legal, regulatory, and compliance obligations (see [Section 10](#10-compliance-controls)).
* Communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests.
* Improve and develop the Services, including through aggregated or de-identified analytics.

> **Note:** We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Role-Based Access to Information

Because the Platform is multi-role, different users can see different subsets of information:

| Role | Can View |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Athlete** | Their own assessments, programs, progress, and messages. |
| **Trainer** | Assigned athletes' assessments, progress, analytics, and messages with those athletes. |
| **Parent/Guardian** | High-level progress summaries for their linked athlete(s); not full assessment responses or private messages unless explicitly shared. |
| **Coach** | High-level progress summaries for athletes on their team; not full assessment responses or private messages unless explicitly shared. |

Access is controlled through role-based permissions. Athletes and/or their guardians control which stakeholders are linked to their account, where applicable.

---

## 5. AI-Generated Program Content

The Platform may use artificial intelligence to help trainers generate training programs. When AI tools are used:
* Inputs (such as assessment results and stated goals) may be processed by AI systems, which may include third-party AI service providers, to produce program recommendations.
* We take steps to limit the data shared with AI providers to what is necessary for this function.
* AI-generated content is reviewed and can be edited by trainers before being assigned to an athlete.
* We do not use athlete assessment data to train third-party AI models without consent, except as necessary to provide the Service itself.

We may share information in the following circumstances:
* **With Other Users:** As described in [Section 4](#4-role-based-access-to-information), based on role and permissions.
* **Service Providers:** Third parties who perform services on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, video conferencing infrastructure, messaging infrastructure, analytics, customer support, and payment processing. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.
* **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.
* **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
* **With Your Consent:** For any other purpose disclosed to you at the time we collect the information, with your consent.

We do not share mental health assessment responses or private messages with parties outside your assigned care team without appropriate authorization, except as required by law or as described above.

## 9. Children's Privacy and Minor Athletes

The Platform is designed to support youth athletes under the supervision of parents, guardians, coaches, and trainers.
* If an athlete is under the age of 18 (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction), a parent or legal guardian must consent to the creation of the athlete's account and the collection of their information.
* For athletes under 13 (in the U.S.) or the applicable age threshold under local law (e.g., under the EU's GDPR or UK GDPR), we comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including obtaining verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information, in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.
* Parents/guardians may review, request correction of, or request deletion of their child's information by contacting us at [support@stepmentalelev8tion.com](mailto:support@stepmentalelev8tion.com).
* Coaches and trainers accessing minor athlete data are expected to comply with our data use policies and applicable youth protection laws.
* We limit data collected from minors to what is reasonably necessary for the Platform's core functionality.
* If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete that information.
